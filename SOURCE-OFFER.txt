WHERE THE SOURCE CODE COMES FROM
--------------------------------
SDR-Tiouner is a modified version of OnAir by adswill and the OnAir contributors:

    https://github.com/adswill/OnAir          (GPL-3.0-or-later)

The receiver, player and signal processing are theirs. The changes made for SDR-Tiouner (the multi-channel receiver and its web page, the
Airspy R2 driver, the stream-out, calibration and load display, the packaging scripts) are NOT in that repository.
